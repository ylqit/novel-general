# longform-novel-engine v0.5.0

v0.5.0 upgrades the engine from contract-complete chapter production to a dual track of factual safety and readable story action.

## Breaking changes

- Adds `story_engine_contract_v1`, `reader_promise_ledger_v1`, and `arc_causal_simulation_v1`; replaces the current chapter protocols with `chapter_contract_v3`, `chapter_direction_candidate_v4`, `chapter_writing_task_v4`, `chapter_story_brief_v2`, and `structure_observation_v2`.
- Removes `information_release` from public chapter protocols without aliases or migration.
- Makes span-backed `scene_prose_editor` review and a four-hash-bound `human_story_review_v2` mandatory before finalization.
- Does not migrate or rewrite v0.4.x novel projects.

## Story production

- Authors receive `chapter_story_brief_v2`, while fact IDs, source hashes, promise IDs, editorial patterns, provenance, RAG, Graph, and SQLite remain internal.
- Rolling plans and structure history track story engine, scene carrier, state change, dramatic method, and exposition carrier.
- Five-chapter diagnostics identify carrier repetition and theme displacement without imposing genre quotas.
- Fanfiction reviews now detect displaced canon events, instrumentalized canon characters, and lost emotional ownership.
- Replaces the public `quality_feedback` concept with the internal `editorial_pattern_item_v1` registry. Only structured review findings may create patterns; current P0/P1 findings still require repair on the same candidate.
- Requires a current causal simulation for direct rolling-outline extension as well as `production next`; the bounded planner context carries the approved causal obligations instead of bypassing them.
- Binds every active Qidian/Fanqie market-profile field to dated `market_evidence_registry_v1` records. Platform-specific cadence remains non-blocking P2 guidance and is never presented as recommendation-algorithm knowledge.

## Human decisions

- `accept` unlocks finalization only for exact candidate, contract, reader-promise-ledger, and causal-simulation hashes plus required prose spans.
- `repair` joins the immutable review bundle and existing two-round repair budget.
- `redirect` preserves the candidate and returns to chapter direction or outline revision through transaction v3.
- Outline revision now owns one transaction-v3 invalidation boundary for reader promises, editorial patterns, causal simulations, chapter cards, author briefs, Agent tasks and SQLite; finalized chapters must be rolled back first.

`blind_review_pack_v3` defines Qidian and Fanqie three-chapter opening scopes plus a fifteen-chapter serial scope. `literary_evidence_ready` is computed only from a valid `literary_evidence_manifest_v1` covering all three; it remains `false` while those real independent reviews are absent.

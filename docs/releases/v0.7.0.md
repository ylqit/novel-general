# longform-novel-engine v0.7.0

v0.7.0 adds a mandatory, evidence-bound human author revision stage before finalization. It is designed to reduce low-quality templating and preserve real human editorial decisions without claiming to evade unpublished platform detectors or guarantee literary quality.

## Breaking changes

- Replaces `human_story_review_v3` with `human_story_review_v4`, bound to the current human revision in addition to candidate, chapter contract, reader-promise ledger, causal simulation, and immutable review-bundle hashes.
- Requires `human_author_revision_v1` and an independent `prose_revision_semantic_reviewer` result for every final candidate.
- Rejects v0.6 human-review projects instead of silently migrating or dual-reading them.

## Human revision and author voice

- Human revision records require exact before/after spans across at least two meaningful dimensions, including scene causality or character voice/emotion. Whitespace-, formatting-, and punctuation-only edits do not satisfy the gate.
- Any later Agent, Humanizer, or repair rewrite invalidates the prior human revision and forces the full review barrier to run again.
- `author_voice_edit_pair_v1` stores only human-approved examples that overlap real revisions and the current final. The first three chapters require one approved pair each before close, and the active bank is capped at 12 with explicit human replacement.

## Low-quality and review safeguards

- Deterministic blocking is limited to directly provable defects such as empty prose, prompt/task leakage, broken formatting, and large exact repetition.
- Isolated words, sentence length, dialogue ratio, sensory density, slow chapters, and hook strength are P2 location signals only. A templating P1 requires multiple exact spans plus evidence of repeated function and reader harm.
- `scene_prose_editor` and `anti_ai_editor` remain mandatory independent reviewers. The local review desk exposes source, human revision, diff, intent, and risk-layered findings without auto-filling human approval reasons.

## Platform publication evidence

- Adds `platform_publication_policy_registry_v1`, non-blocking `publication preflight`, `publication_risk_report_v2`, and `creation_provenance_manifest_v1`.
- Fanqie checks map only to publicly documented low-quality governance categories. Qidian checks explicitly state that no verifiable public blanket AI ban or internal detection method is known.
- Publication reports never output AI probability, detector-passing claims, evasion advice, or a human-writing percentage, and they do not guarantee platform acceptance.

Protocol and author-acceptance readiness remain separate from literary evidence. This release contains no completed cross-model, cross-genre independent blind-review manifests, so `literary_evidence_ready=false`.

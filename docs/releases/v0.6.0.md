# longform-novel-engine v0.6.0

v0.6.0 turns the Qidian male profile into the primary market contract and adds a mandatory, evidence-bound human deep-review boundary before finalization.

## Breaking changes

- Replaces the current human decision protocol with `human_story_review_v3`, bound to candidate, chapter contract, reader-promise ledger, causal simulation, and immutable review-bundle hashes.
- Replaces the market evidence schema with `market_evidence_registry_v2`.
- Requires 2–3 stable option IDs and a `chapter_direction_selection_v1` sidecar before direction approval and semantic compilation.
- Does not migrate old human review or direction-selection records.

## Human review and repair

- Accept requires all ten review dimensions and exact spans for the key turn, character choice/emotion, and reader gain.
- Repair annotations carry span, dimension, P0/P1/P2 severity, action, intent, and protected outcomes. Redirect explicitly returns to chapter direction or outline revision.
- `human_review_advisor` consultations remain non-canonical; candidate changes make prior consultation sessions stale.
- Human prose editing is limited to a complete candidate for a validated repair task and reruns the entire gate and independent-review barrier.

## Local review desk and quality status

- Adds `review serve`, a loopback-only three-column desk with one-time token bootstrap, Host/Origin/CSRF/CSP validation, output escaping, expected hashes, and project locks.
- Adds `quality status`, reporting `protocol_ready`, `author_acceptance_ready`, and `literary_evidence_ready` independently.
- Qidian is the primary contract. Fanqie compatibility remains non-blocking P2 observation and does not claim knowledge of recommendation algorithms.

Literary evidence now requires matched v0.6.0 candidate and v0.5.0 baseline runs across all three blind-review scopes. Until independent review manifests exist, `literary_evidence_ready=false`.

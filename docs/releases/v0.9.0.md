# longform-novel-engine v0.9.0

v0.9.0 makes human intent and human final revision mandatory parts of the chapter lifecycle. It focuses on reducing templated, causally weak, and mechanically repetitive prose without claiming to evade unpublished platform detection or guarantee acceptance by Qidian or Fanqie.

## Breaking changes

- Requires a blank, human-authored `human_chapter_intent_v1` before Story Brief compilation.
- Upgrades the author context chain to `chapter_story_brief_basis_v2`, `chapter_story_brief_v4`, and `chapter_writing_task_v6`.
- Requires `human_author_revision_v3`, `human_final_lock_v1`, and `human_story_review_v6` before finalization.
- Rejects v0.8 and earlier project evidence instead of silently migrating, aliasing, or dual-reading it.
- Renames the public review roles to `anti_template_editor` and `prose_naturalness`; retired names are actionable tombstones rather than aliases.

## Human-led coediting

- `chapter_coedit_session_v1` stores selected spans, two or three advisory options, the human choice, intent, and complete workbench candidate hashes.
- Coediting never writes draft, final, or canonical data directly. Existing P0/P1 findings still require the immutable repair plan and two-round budget.
- After the human final is locked, AI may only provide read-only consultation. Any later AI prose transformation invalidates the lock and requires another human final revision and complete review pass.

## Anti-template quality controls

- Deterministic blocking remains limited to directly provable defects such as empty prose, prompt leakage, broken formatting, and large exact repetition.
- Common words, sentence length, dialogue ratio, slow chapters, sensory density, and non-cliffhanger endings remain P2 location signals only.
- A templating P1 requires multiple exact spans, repeated narrative function, reader harm, and protected content.
- Recent structural fingerprints are bounded observations; historical findings cannot automatically condemn a new chapter.

## Platform and literary evidence boundary

- Qidian and Fanqie share the core chapter contract for desire, resistance, choice, cost, state change, reader gain, and long-term payoff. Platform differences remain non-blocking P2 diagnostics outside the author prompt.
- Publication reports do not output AI probability, detector-passing claims, evasion advice, or human-writing percentages.
- The repository does not contain the required independent human blind-review manifest, so `literary_evidence_ready=false`.
